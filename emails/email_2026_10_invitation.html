We invite you to come back and look at Earth at night

Hello,

You took part in the old Lost at Night, which is why we are writing to you. This is the only email we will send you: we have not added you to any mailing list and we will not write again unless you decide to join.

The project is back and renewed at lostatnight.org. More than 30,000 control points have already been placed on night-time photos from the International Space Station, and there is plenty new: year-long challenges open until October 2027 (Europe, Spain and France), teams to play with your class or friends, a public ranking and thousands of new photos.

Accounts from the old site were not migrated, so you will need to create a new one (just an email address, or sign in with Google).
