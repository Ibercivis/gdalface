Hi [first name],

Thank you for being part of Lost at Night. Every image you geolocate becomes a validated data point that researchers use to study light pollution, biodiversity, public health and energy policy. Without contributors like you, those images would still be sitting unlocated on a server somewhere — so, simply: thank you.

We wanted to invite you to something a bit different from the usual solo session in front of the screen.

On 21 May 2026, at 12:00 CEST, we are running an online community workshop. The plan is simple: we get together for 90 minutes, two members of the team share a bit of what is happening behind the scenes (what the science team is doing with our data, and what is new on the development side), and then we geolocate together, live.

Our collective goal

3,000 control points added live during the session.

Easily within reach if enough of us show up.

It is also a chance to ask whatever you have always wanted to ask — about the images, the science, the tool, or where the project is heading.
