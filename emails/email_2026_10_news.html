What's new · October 2026

The night just got a bit more fun.

Three new year-long challenges, teams you can form with your class, club or friends, and a public ranking to follow it all.

Hi [first name],

Thank you for helping us locate the Earth at night. Over 30,000 control points have been placed so far, and we have been busy making the platform better for everyone who keeps coming back. Here is what changed.

1 · New challenges

Europe, Spain and France — open until October 2027

Three region challenges are open for a whole year. Join one and every photo you locate inside its region counts towards a shared bronze, silver and gold goal. You can be in one challenge at a time and switch whenever you like; your points stay with you.

Pick a challenge

2 · Teams

Play together: classrooms, clubs, friends

Create a team, share its invite link, and everything your members locate while in the team adds up on the team ranking. Open teams accept anyone; invite-only teams need the link. Teachers: this is made for you.

Create or join a team

3 · Ranking

See where you stand — this week, this month, all time

The ranking is now public: people, teams and countries, by control points, tasks or time, over the last 7 days, 30 days or all time. Your own row is highlighted, and your area links straight to it.

Find my position

4 · Simpler

One button, and new photos on the way

No more choosing a photo set: Participate serves you the next photo, and your challenge, if you have one, narrows it down for you. Thousands of new night photos from the International Space Station are being loaded this week.
